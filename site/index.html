Features

  • Every app, not just one browser

    Ads and trackers are blocked for every browser and app on your PC. There is no extension to install.

  • Cookie pop-ups

    Annoying cookie privacy popups are closed automatically.

  • YouTube ads blocked

    Watch YouTube without annoying ads.

  • Trackers blocked

    Trackers disguised as part of the site you're on are recognised and blocked.

  • Link cleaner

    Tracking tags and redirects are stripped clean so no one can track you when you click a link.

  • Third-party cookies stripped

    Trackers embedded in other sites can't set or read cookies to follow you around the web.

  • Turn off Google's AI Overview

How it works

AdBlitz runs on your PC. Windows sends your web traffic through it, and it stops ads, trackers and dangerous sites before they reach your browser or apps.

It checks every request

Every request your PC makes is checked against more than 160,000 rules for ads, trackers and malware, updated every few hours. Each check takes a few microseconds, so you won't notice it.

Your browsing stays on your PC

There's no account and no AdBlitz server. Filtering happens on your computer, and your statistics stay on it.

Set up once

Install it and forget it. AdBlitz starts with Windows and keeps its protection up to date on its own.
